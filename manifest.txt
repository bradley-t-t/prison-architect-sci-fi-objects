Name                 "Sci-Fi Objects"
Author               Knight
Description          "Laser doors look odd next to a pine desk. Sci-Fi Objects gives the furniture and equipment of the whole prison a clean futuristic finish, in 2D and in 3D, to match Sci-Fi Security.\r\n\r\n[h2]How to use it[/h2]\r\n[olist]\r\n[*]Subscribe, turn the mod on in Extras > Mods, and load your prison. There is nothing to build and nothing to set.\r\n[*]Old saves change over the moment they load.\r\n[*]To go back, turn the mod off. Everything returns to how the game drew it.\r\n[/olist]\r\n\r\n[h2]What you get[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]Wooden table tops, desks, shelves and sheds become a light titanium composite. The grain, the shading and everything sitting on the surface stay.\r\n[*]Beds, bunks, double beds and medical beds become sleep capsules with a headrest, rows of light tubes, a raised smoked lid and a small console at the foot. Each takes its accent colour from its own bedding.\r\n[*]Toilets, sinks and shower heads become clean white composite with every fitting kept, and a toilet bowl is dark glass with a line of light round its rim.\r\n[*]Canteen tables, benches and serving tables become one sheet of brushed steel, and the cooker becomes induction rings of black glass.\r\n[*]The autopsy table becomes brushed steel and keeps its sink, tap, grating and stains.\r\n[*]Screens the game drew, such as monitor walls and arcade machines, light up in their own colour with scanlines.\r\n[*]Green and blue fabric, such as baize and upholstery, keeps its colour at a quieter strength and the same brightness.\r\n[*]Doors become powered two-leaf plates with a milled seam, a recessed panel in each leaf, chevrons at the leading edges and one status lamp. A door painted for a reason keeps that colour, so a staff door is still green and a visiting door still maroon, and a wooden one becomes steel.\r\n[*]Cell doors keep their openings and their grade colour, and every bar becomes a machined mullion lit down its middle, set in a graphite rail with a lock block.\r\n[*]Drains become a milled steel collar round a sunken well.\r\n[*]Built floors and walls match. Concrete, tiles and paving go a clean neutral grey, and wood, brick, mosaic and iron floors become composite with their boards and patterns kept. Security walls keep their colour bands.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]What stays the same[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]Colour that means something. Staff, remote and jail doors keep their colours, and so do fence gates, traffic lights, crosses, labels and warning marks.\r\n[*]Ice, water and anything else pale blue.\r\n[*]The keycard door, which is already the future. The ornamental gates and mesh fence gates, which are openwork a solid plate would fill in. The secret door, which goes on passing for a bookshelf.\r\n[*]Grass, dirt, sand, water, roads and every other natural tile.\r\n[*]Prisoners, staff, dogs, plants, trees, food, fire, contraband, tools, weapons, uniforms and the game's signs and icons.\r\n[/list]\r\nThe mod never darkens anything past what the game drew and never draws over an object, so you can still read your prison at a glance.\r\n\r\n[h2]What changes in your game[/h2]\r\nOnly how things look. It touches no object, price, room, need or rule, so you can add it to or remove it from any save.\r\n\r\n[h2]Conflicts[/h2]\r\nThe game keeps one copy of each object sheet, the one from the last mod in your load order that ships one. Any other mod that replaces the game's object art either overrides this one or is overridden by it. Mods that add their own new objects keep their own look, and that includes Sci-Fi Security, Crematorium, Auto Hire, Robot Staff and Contraband Checkpoint Scanner. The walls and floors live in one tileset file that Sci-Fi Security also ships, and both mods carry the same copy, so the laser fences and the new floors work together in either load order."
