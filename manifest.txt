Name                 "Sci-Fi Objects"
Author               Knight
Description          "Re-materials the furniture and equipment of the whole prison in a clean futuristic finish, in 2D and in 3D, to sit beside Sci-Fi Security. Wooden tables, desks, shelves and plain doors become a light titanium composite that keeps the grain and everything sitting on it. Beds become sleep capsules with rows of light tubes, the autopsy table becomes brushed steel, and the screens the game drew are lit. Colour that means something stays: staff, remote and jail doors keep their colours, and so do crosses, labels and warning marks. People, animals, plants, food and tools are drawn as they always were. It changes how things look and nothing else."
