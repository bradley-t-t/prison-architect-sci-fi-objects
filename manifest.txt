Name                 "Sci-Fi Objects"
Author               Knight
Description          "Re-materials the furniture and equipment of the whole prison in a clean futuristic finish, in 2D and in 3D, to sit beside Sci-Fi Security. Wooden tables, desks, shelves and plain doors become a light titanium composite that keeps the grain and everything sitting on it. Beds become sleep capsules with rows of light tubes, the autopsy table becomes brushed steel, and it lights the screens the game drew. Most doors become powered two-leaf plates with a milled seam and a status lamp, keeping any colour that says who may use them, cell doors become machined mullions in a graphite rail, and drains become milled steel. Built floors and walls follow: concrete and tile go neutral, wood, brick and iron become composite with their pattern kept, and it leaves grass, dirt and water alone. Colour that means something stays: staff, remote and jail doors keep their colours, and so do crosses, labels and warning marks. People, animals, plants, food and tools look as they always did. It changes how things look and nothing else."
